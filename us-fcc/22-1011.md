## Important Information

- The antenna height of offshore stations must not exceed 61 meters (200 feet) above mean sea level.
- The antenna height of offshore surface mobile stations must not exceed 10 meters (30 feet) above the waterline.

## Full Text

§ 22.1011 Antenna height limitations. The antenna height of offshore stations must not exceed 61 meters (200 feet) above mean sea level. The antenna height of offshore surface mobile stations must not exceed 10 meters (30 feet) above the waterline.
