## Full Text

§ 73.2090 Ban on discrimination in broadcast transactions. No qualified person or entity shall be discriminated against on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin or sex in the sale of commercially operated AM, FM, TV, Class A TV or international broadcast stations (as defined in this part). [73 FR 28369, May 16, 2008]
