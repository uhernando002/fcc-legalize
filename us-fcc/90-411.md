## Important Information

- § 90.411 Civil defense communications.
- The licensee of any station authorized under this part may, on a voluntary basis, transmit communications necessary for the implementation of civil defense activities assigned such station by local civil defense authorities during an actual or simulated emergency, including drills and tests.
- The Commission may at any time order the discontinuance of such special use of the authorized facilities.
