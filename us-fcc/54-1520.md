## Important Information

- A recipient of transitional mobile support shall commit to, at a minimum, maintaining its network coverage area as of June 30, 2023, or 100 percent of its network coverage area prior to Hurricanes Maria and Irma as specified by § 54.1514(a), whichever is greater.
- The recipient shall also commit to provide a minimum level of service that meets or exceeds network levels and at reasonably comparable levels to those services and rates available in urban areas as required by § 54.1521(a).

## Full Text

§ 54.1520 Provision of transitional mobile support. A recipient of transitional mobile support shall commit to, at a minimum, maintaining its network coverage area as of June 30, 2023, or 100 percent of its network coverage area prior to Hurricanes Maria and Irma as specified by § 54.1514(a), whichever is greater. The recipient shall also commit to provide a minimum level of service that meets or exceeds network levels and at reasonably comparable levels to those services and rates available in urban areas as required by § 54.1521(a).
