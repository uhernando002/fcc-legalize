## Important Information

- § 101.1101 LMDS service subject to competitive bidding.
- Mutually exclusive initial applications for LMDS licenses are subject to competitive bidding procedures.
- The general competitive bidding procedures set forth in part 1, subpart Q of this chapter will apply unless otherwise provided in this subpart.
