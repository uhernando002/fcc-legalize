## Full Text

§ 73.88 Blanketing interference. The licensee of each broadcast station is required to satisfy all reasonable complaints of blanketing interference within the 1 V/m contour. Note: For more detailed instructions concerning operational responsibilities of licensees and permittees under this section, see § 73.318 (b), (c) and (d).
