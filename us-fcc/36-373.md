## Full Text

§ 36.373 Services—Account 6620. (a) For apportionment purposes, the expenses in this account are first segregated on the basis of an analysis of job functions into the following classifications: Telephone operator services: publishing directory listing; and all other. (1) Expenses may be apportioned among the operations for groups of exchanges. A group of exchanges may include all exchanges in the study area.
