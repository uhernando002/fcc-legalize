§ 61.68 Special notations. (a) Any tariff filing made pursuant to an Application for Special Permission, Commission decision or order must contain the following statement: Issued under authority of (specific reference to the special permission, Commission decision, or order) of the Commission. (b) When a portion of any tariff publication is issued in order to comply with the Commission order, the following notation must be associated with that portion of the tariff publication: In compliance with the order of the Federal Communications Commission in _ (a specific citation to the applicable order should be made).
