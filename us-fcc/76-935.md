## Important Information

- In order to regulate basic service tier rates or associated equipment costs, a franchising authority must have procedural laws or regulations applicable to rate regulation proceedings that provide a reasonable opportunity for consideration of the views of interested parties.
- Such rules must take into account the time periods that franchising authorities have to review rates under § 76.933.
