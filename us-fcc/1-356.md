## Important Information

- No document or exhibit, or part thereof, shall be received as, or admitted in, evidence unless offered in duplicate.
- In addition, when exhibits of a documentary character are to be offered in evidence, copies shall be furnished to other counsel unless the presiding officer otherwise directs.
