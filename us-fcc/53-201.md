## Important Information

- § 53.201 Services for which a section 272 affiliate is required.
- When providing previously authorized activities described in section 271(f) of the Act, a BOC shall comply with the following: (1) A BOC shall provide previously authorized interLATA information services and manufacturing activities through a section 272 affiliate no later than February 8, 1997.
- (2) A BOC shall provide previously authorized interLATA telecommunications services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the orders entered by the United States District Court for the District of Columbia pursuant to section VII or VIII(C) of the AT&T Consent Decree that authorized such services.
- A BOC shall provide an interLATA information service through a section 272 affiliate when it provides the interLATA telecommunications transmission component of the service either over its own facilities, or by reselling the interLATA telecommunications services of an interexchange provider.
- A BOC shall provide out-of-region interLATA information services through a section 272 affiliate.

## Full Text

§ 53.201 Services for which a section 272 affiliate is required. For the purposes of applying section 272(a)(2) of the Act: (a) Previously authorized activities. When providing previously authorized activities described in section 271(f) of the Act, a BOC shall comply with the following: (1) A BOC shall provide previously authorized interLATA information services and manufacturing activities through a section 272 affiliate no later than February 8, 1997. (2) A BOC shall provide previously authorized interLATA telecommunications services in accordance with the terms and conditions of the orders entered by the United States District Court for the District of Columbia pursuant to section VII or VIII(C) of the AT&T Consent Decree that authorized such services. (b) InterLATA information services. A BOC shall provide an interLATA information service through a section 272 affiliate when it provides the interLATA telecommunications transmission component of the service either over its own facilities, or by reselling the interLATA telecommunications services of an interexchange provider. (c) Out-of-region interLATA information services. A BOC shall provide out-of-region interLATA information services through a section 272 affiliate.
