## Important Information

- § 101.1511 Regulatory status and eligibility.
- (a) Licensees are permitted to provide services on a non-common carrier and/or on a common carrier basis.
- (b) Licensees are subject to the requirements set forth in § 101.7.
- (c) Any entity, other than one precluded by § 101.7, is eligible for authorization to provide service under this part.
- Authorization will be granted upon proper application filing and link coordination in accordance with the Commission's rules.
