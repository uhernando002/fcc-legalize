## Important Information

- § 22.705 Rural radiotelephone system configuration.
- Stations in the Rural Radiotelephone Service are authorized to communicate as follows: (a) Rural subscriber stations are authorized to communicate with and through the central office station(s) with which they are associated.
- However, where the establishment of a central office station in this service is not feasible, rural subscriber stations may be authorized to communicate with and through a base station in the Paging and Radiotelephone Service.
- (b) Central office stations may communicate only with rural subscriber stations.
- (c) Interoffice stations may communicate only with other interoffice stations.
