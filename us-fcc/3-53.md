## Full Text

§ 3.53 FCC notification of refusal to provide telecommunications service to U.S. registered vessel(s). An accounting authority must inform the FCC immediately should it receive notice from any source that a foreign administration or facility is refusing or plans to refuse legitimate public correspondence to or from any U.S. registered vessel.
