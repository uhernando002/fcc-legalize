## Important Information

- § 80.771 Method of computing coverage.
- Compute the + 17 dBu contour as follows: (a) Determine the effective antenna height above mean sea level according to the procedures in §§ 80.757-80.761.
- (b) Determine the effective radiated power according to § 80,765.
- Determine for each radial the distance from the antenna site to the + 17 dBu point of field strength using procedures of §§ 80.765 and 80.767.
- (c) Plot on a suitable map each point of + 17 dBu field strength for all radials and draw the contour by connecting the adjacent points by a smooth curve.
