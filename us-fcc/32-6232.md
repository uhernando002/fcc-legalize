§ 32.6232 Circuit equipment expense. (a) This account shall include expenses associated with circuit equipment. Circuit equipment expenses shall be maintained in the following subaccounts: 6232.1 Electronic, 6232.2 Optical. (b) This subaccount 6232.1 Electronic shall include expenses associated with electronic circuit equipment. (c) This subaccount 6232.2 Optical shall include expenses associated with optical circuit equipment. [67 FR 5695, Feb. 6, 2002]
