## Important Information

- § 0.414 The Communications Act and other statutory materials.
- This publication, with packets of revised pages, contains the Communications Act of 1934, with amendments through 1964; the Administrative Procedure Act, with amendments through 1964; the Judicial Review Act; the Communications Satellite Act of 1962; and selected sections of the Criminal Code pertaining to communications.
- It also contains indexes to the Communications Act and the Administrative Procedure Act.
- Persons who do not have ready access to the United States Code, or who refer frequently to these materials, may find this volume to be useful.
