## Full Text

§ 73.6025 Antenna system and station location. (a) Applications for modified Class A TV facilities proposing the use of directional antenna systems must include all appropriate documentation specified in § 73.625(c)(3). (b) [Reserved] (c) Where a Class A TV licensee or permittee proposes to mount its antenna on or near an AM tower, as defined in § 1.30002, the Class A TV licensee or permittee must comply with § 1.30003 or § 1.30002. (d) Class A TV stations are subject to the provisions in § 73.617(d) regarding blanketing interference.
