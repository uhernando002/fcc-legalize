## Full Text

§ 76.952 Information to be provided by cable operator on monthly subscriber bills. All cable operators must provide the following information to subscribers on monthly bills: (a) The name, mailing address and phone number of the franchising authority, unless the franchising authority in writing requests the cable operator to omit such information. (b) The FCC community unit identifier for the cable system. [58 FR 29753, May 21, 1993, as amended at 59 FR 17960, Apr. 15, 1994; 64 FR 35950, July 2, 1999]
