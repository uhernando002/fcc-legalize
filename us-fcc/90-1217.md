## Important Information

- The 4.9 GHz Band Manager will have the following primary responsibilities: (a) Frequency coordination and interference protection for 4.9 GHz band incumbent public safety operations; (b) Incentivizing the use of the latest commercially available technologies, including 5G; (c) Facilitating non-public safety use of the 4.9 GHz band.
- (d) Managing a sharing agreement with the First Responders Network Authority pursuant to §§ 90.1207(h) and 2.103(b) of this chapter; and (e) Filing an annual report with the Commission.

§ 90.1217 4.9 GHz Band Manager. (d) Managing a sharing agreement with the First Responders Network Authority pursuant to §§ 90.1207(h) and 2.103(b) of this chapter; and (e) Filing an annual report with the Commission.
