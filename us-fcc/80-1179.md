## Important Information

- When an on-board repeater is used, the following limitations must be met: (a) The on-board repeater antenna must be located no higher than 3 meters (10 feet) above the vessel's highest working deck.
- (b) Each on-board repeater must have a timer that deactivates the transmitter if the carrier remains on for more than 3 minutes.

## Full Text

§ 80.1179 On-board repeater limitations. When an on-board repeater is used, the following limitations must be met: (a) The on-board repeater antenna must be located no higher than 3 meters (10 feet) above the vessel's highest working deck. (b) Each on-board repeater must have a timer that deactivates the transmitter if the carrier remains on for more than 3 minutes.
