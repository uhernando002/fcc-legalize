## Important Information

- (a) Each responsible party for equipment that is subject to a Supplier's Declaration of Conformity under this part, shall compile a description of the measurement facilities employed for testing the equipment.
- The responsible party for the Supplier's Declaration of Conformity shall retain a description of the measurement facilities.
- (b) The description shall contain the information required to be included by the Administrative Council for Terminal Attachments.

§ 68.346 Description of testing facilities.
