## Important Information

- Each COLEM recovering fees from examinees must maintain records of expenses and revenues, frequency of examinations administered, and examination pass rates.
- Records must cover the period from January 1 to December 31 of the preceding year and must be submitted as directed by the Commission.
- Each COLEM must retain records for 3 years and the records must be made available to the FCC upon request.
