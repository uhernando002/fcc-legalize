§ 30.4 Frequencies. The following frequencies are available for assignment in the Upper Microwave Flexible Use Service: (a) 24.25-24.45 GHz and 24.75-25.25 GHz bands—24.25-24.35 GHz; 24.35-24.45 GHz; 24.75-24.85 GHz; 24.85-24.95 GHz; 24.95-25.05 GHz; 25.05-25.15 GHz; and 25.15-25.25 GHz. (b) [Reserved] (c) 27.5 GHz—28.35 GHz band—27.5-27.925 GHz and 27.925-28.35 GHz. (d) 38.6-40 GHz band: (1) New channel plan: Channel No. Frequency bandlimits(MHz) 1 38,600-38,700 2 38,700-38,800 3 38,800-38,900 4 38,900-39,000 5 39,000-39,100 6 39,100-39,200 7 39,200-39,300 8 39,300-39,400 9 39,400-39,500 10 39,500-39,600 11 39,600-39,700 12 39,700-39,800 13 39,800-39,900 14 39,900-40,000 (2) [Reserved] (e) [Reserved] (f) 37-38.6 GHz band: This band is divided into the Lower 37 GHz band and the Upper 37 GHz band. Lower 37 GHz band channels: 37,000-37,100 MHz, 37,100-37,200 MHz, 37,200-37,300 MHz, 37, 300-37,400 MHz, 37,400-37,500 MHz, 37,500-37,600 MHz. The 37,000-37,600 MHz band segment shall be available on a site-specific, coordinated shared basis with eligible Federal entities. Upper 37 GHz band channels: 37,600-37,700; 37,700-37,800 MHz; 37,800-37,900 MHz; 37,900-38,000 MHz; 38,000-38,100 MHz; 38,100-38,200 MHz; 38,200-38,300 MHz; 38,300-38,400 MHz; 38,400-38,500 MHz, and 38,500-38,600 MHz. (g) 47.2-48.2 GHz band—47.2-47.3 GHz; 47.3-47.4 GHz; 47.4-47.5 GHz; 47.5-47.6 GHz; 47.6-47.7 GHz; 47.7-47.8 GHz; 47.8-47.9 GHz; 47.9-48.0 GHz; 48.0-48.1 GHz; and 48.1-48.2 GHz.
