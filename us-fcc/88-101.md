## Full Text

§ 88.101 Transmitter power. Transmitters operating in the 5030-5091 MHz band must comply with the transmitter output power specified in technical standard RTCA DO-362A (incorporated by reference, see § 88.117) section 2.2.1.6.1 and associated subsections.
