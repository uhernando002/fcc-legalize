## Important Information

- Beginning April 1, 1989, such expenses shall be assigned to the Lifeline Assistance element.

## Full Text

§ 69.414 Lifeline assistance expenses. Expenses allocated to the interstate jurisdiction pursuant to § 36.741 shall be assigned to the Carrier Common Line element until March 31, 1989. Beginning April 1, 1989, such expenses shall be assigned to the Lifeline Assistance element.
