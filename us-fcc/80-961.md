## Important Information

- (a) The receiver must be capable of reception of G3E emission on the required frequencies.
- (b) The receiver must have a sensitivity of at least 2 microvolts across 50 ohms for a 20 decibel signal-to-noise ratio.

§ 80.961 Radiotelephone receiver. (a) The receiver must be capable of reception of G3E emission on the required frequencies. (b) The receiver must have a sensitivity of at least 2 microvolts across 50 ohms for a 20 decibel signal-to-noise ratio.
