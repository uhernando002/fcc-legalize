## Important Information

- No person shall use a Personal Radio Service station: (a) In connection with any activity which is against Federal, State or local law; (b) To transmit advertisements or program material associated with television or radio broadcasting; (c) To transmit messages for hire or provide a common carrier service; (d) To intentionally interfere with the communications of another station; (e) To transmit obscene, profane or indecent words, language or meaning; or (f) To transmit a false or deceptive communication.
