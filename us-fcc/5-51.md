## Important Information

- (a) Authorizations for stations in the Experimental Radio Service will be issued only to persons qualified to conduct the types of operations permitted in § 5.3, including testing laboratories recognized by the Commission for radio frequency device testing.
- (b) No foreign government or representative thereof is eligible to hold a station license in the Experimental Radio Service.

§ 5.51 Eligibility. (a) Authorizations for stations in the Experimental Radio Service will be issued only to persons qualified to conduct the types of operations permitted in § 5.3, including testing laboratories recognized by the Commission for radio frequency device testing. (b) No foreign government or representative thereof is eligible to hold a station license in the Experimental Radio Service.
