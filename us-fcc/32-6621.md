## Full Text

§ 32.6621 Call completion services. This account shall include costs incurred in helping customers place and complete calls, except directory assistance. This includes handling and recording; intercept; quoting rates, time and charges; and all other activities involved in the manual handling of calls. [69 FR 44607, July 27, 2004]
