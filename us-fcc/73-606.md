## Important Information

- Channels designated with an asterisk are assigned for use by noncommercial educational broadcast stations only.

§ 73.606 Table of allotments. The table of allotments set forth in § 73.622(j) contains the channels designated for the listed communities in the United States, its Territories, and possessions.
