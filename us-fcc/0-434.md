## Important Information

- § 0.434 Data bases and lists of authorized broadcast stations and pending broadcast applications.
- The FCC makes available its data bases, Consolidated Database System (CDBS) and Licensing and Management System (LMS), containing information about authorized broadcast stations, pending applications for such stations, and rulemaking proceedings involving amendments to the TV and FM Table of Allotments.
- CDBS and LMS contain frequencies, station locations, and other particulars.
- CDBS and LMS may be viewed at the Commission's website at www.fcc.gov under Media Bureau.
