## Important Information

- When transmitting, each amateur station must have a control operator.
- The control operator must be a person: (a) For whom an amateur operator/primary station license grant appears on the ULS consolidated licensee database, or (b) Who is authorized for alien reciprocal operation by § 97.107 of this part.
