## Important Information

- (a) C-V2X OBUs must comply with the following out-of-band emissions limits.
- Conducted emissions limits measured at the antenna input shall not exceed: (1) −16 dBm/100 kHz within ±1 megahertz of the band edges; (2) −13 dBm/MHz within ±1 megahertz to ±5 megahertz of the band edges; (3) −16 dBm/MHz within ±5 megahertz to ±30 megahertz of the band edges; and (4) −28 dBm/MHz beyond 30 megahertz from the band edges.
