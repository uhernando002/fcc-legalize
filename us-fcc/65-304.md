§ 65.304 Capital structure. The proportion of each cost of capital component in the capital structure is equal to: Proportion in the capital structure = Where: “Book Value of particular component” is the total of the book values of that component for all local exchange carriers with annual revenues equal to or above the indexed revenue threshold as defined in § 32.9000. “Book Value of Debt + Book Value of Preferred Stock + Book Value of Equity” is the total of the book values of all the components for all local exchange carriers with annual revenues equal to or above the indexed revenue threshold as defined in § 32.9000.
