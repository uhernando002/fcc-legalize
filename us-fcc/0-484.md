## Important Information

- Generally, examinations for amateur radio operation licenses shall be administered at locations and times specified by volunteer examiners.
- When the FCC conducts examinations for amateur radio operator licenses, they shall take place at locations and times designated by the FCC.

## Full Text

§ 0.484 Amateur radio operator examinations. Generally, examinations for amateur radio operation licenses shall be administered at locations and times specified by volunteer examiners. (See § 97.509). When the FCC conducts examinations for amateur radio operator licenses, they shall take place at locations and times designated by the FCC. [58 FR 13021, Mar. 9, 1993]
