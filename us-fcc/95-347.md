## Full Text

§ 95.347 Automatic control. Operation of Personal Radio Services stations under automatic control is prohibited, unless otherwise allowed for a particular Personal Radio Service by rules in the subpart governing that specific service. See, e.g., §§ 95.1747, 95.2347, 95.2547, 95.3347. [82 FR 43871, Sept. 20, 2017]
