## Important Information

- § 27.9 Operation of certificated signal boosters.
- Individuals and non-individuals may operate certificated Consumer Signal Boosters on frequencies regulated under this part provided that such operation complies with all applicable rules under this part and § 20.21 of this chapter.
- Failure to comply with all applicable rules voids the authority to operate a signal booster.
