## Full Text

§ 80.69 Facilities requirement for public coast stations using telephony. Public coast stations using telephony must be provided with the following facilities. (a) When the station is authorized to use frequencies in the 1605-3500 kHz band, equipment meeting the requirements of § 80.67(b) must be installed at each transmitting location. (b) The transmitter power on the frequency 2182 kHz must not exceed 50 watts carrier power for normal operation. During distress, urgency and safety traffic, operation at maximum power is permitted.
