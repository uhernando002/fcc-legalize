## Important Information

- The EAJA applies to any adversary adjudication pending or commenced before the Commission on or after August 5, 1985.
- The provisions of § 1.1505(b) apply to any adversary adjudications commenced on or after March 29, 1996.

## Full Text

§ 1.1502 When the EAJA applies. The EAJA applies to any adversary adjudication pending or commenced before the Commission on or after August 5, 1985. The provisions of § 1.1505(b) apply to any adversary adjudications commenced on or after March 29, 1996. [61 FR 39898, July 31, 1996]
