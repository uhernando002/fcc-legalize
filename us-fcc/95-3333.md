## Important Information

- § 95.3333 Airborne use of 76-81 GHz Band Radar Service is prohibited.
- Notwithstanding the provisions of § 95.3331, 76-81 GHz Band Radar Service is prohibited aboard aircraft in flight.
- Aircraft-mounted radars shall be equipped with a mechanism that will prevent operations once the aircraft becomes airborne.
