## Important Information

- § 1.926 Application processing; initial procedures.
- Applications are assigned file numbers and service codes in order to facilitate processing.
- Assignment of a file number to an application is for administrative convenience and does not constitute a determination that the application is acceptable for filing.
- Purpose and service codes appear on the Commission forms.
