## Full Text

§ 95.3305 Radar operator eligibility in the 76-81 GHz Band. Subject to the requirements of §§ 95.305 and 95.307, any person is eligible to operate a radar in the 76-81 GHz band without an individual license; such operation must comply with all applicable rules in this subpart.
