## Full Text

§ 73.687 Transmission system requirements. (a)-(b) [Reserved] (c) Requirements applicable to transmitters are as follows: (1) [Reserved] (2) The transmitters shall be equipped with suitable indicating instruments for the determination of operating power and with other instruments necessary for proper adjustment, operation, and maintenance of the equipment. (3) Adequate provision shall be made for varying the output power of the transmitters to compensate for excessive variations in line voltage or for other factors affecting the output power. (4) Adequate provisions shall be provided in all component parts to avoid overheating at the rated maximum output powers. (d) The construction, installation, and operation of broadcast equipment is expected to conform with all applicable local, state, and federally imposed safety regulations and standards, enforcement of which is the responsibility of the issuing regulatory agency.
