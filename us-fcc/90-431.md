## Important Information

- No person is required to be in attendance at a station when transmitting during normal rendition of service and when either: (a) Transmitting for telemetering purposes; or, (b) Retransmitting by self-actuating means a radio signal received from another radio station or stations.

§ 90.431 Unattended operation. No person is required to be in attendance at a station when transmitting during normal rendition of service and when either: (a) Transmitting for telemetering purposes; or, (b) Retransmitting by self-actuating means a radio signal received from another radio station or stations.
