## Important Information

- § 73.1015 Truthful written statements and responses to Commission inquiries and correspondence.
- The Commission or its representatives may, in writing, require from any applicant, permittee, or licensee written statements of fact relevant to a determination whether an application should be granted or denied, or to a determination whether a license should be revoked, or to any other matter within the jurisdiction of the Commission, or, in the case of a proceeding to amend the Table of FM Allotments or Table of TV Allotments, require from any person filing an expression of interest, written statements of fact relevant to that allotment proceeding.
- Any such statements of fact are subject to the provisions of § 1.17 of this chapter.
