## Important Information

- No international broadcast station shall be authorized to install, or be licensed for operation of, transmitter equipment with: (a) A rated carrier power of less than 50 kilowatts (kW) if double-sideband (DSB) modulation is used, (b) A peak envelope power of less than 50 kW if single-sideband (SSB) modulation is used, or (c) A mean power of less than 10 kW if digital modulation is used.

§ 73.751 Operating power. No international broadcast station shall be authorized to install, or be licensed for operation of, transmitter equipment with: (a) A rated carrier power of less than 50 kilowatts (kW) if double-sideband (DSB) modulation is used, (b) A peak envelope power of less than 50 kW if single-sideband (SSB) modulation is used, or (c) A mean power of less than 10 kW if digital modulation is used.
