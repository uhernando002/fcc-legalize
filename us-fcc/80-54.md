## Important Information

- § 80.54 Automated Maritime Telecommunications System (AMTS)—System Licensing.
- AMTS licensees will be issued blanket authority for a system of coast stations and mobile units (subscribers).
- AMTS applicants will specify the maximum number of mobile units to be placed in operation during the license period.
