## Full Text

§ 64.1204 Private entity submissions of robocall violations. (a) Any private entity may submit to the Enforcement Bureau information related to a call made or a text message sent that the private entity has reason to believe was in violation of § 64.1200(a) or 47 U.S.C. 227(b). (b) For the purposes of this section, the term “private entity” shall mean any entity other than a natural individual person or a public entity.
