## Important Information

- In order to be eligible to bid, each pending applicant must timely submit certain supplemental information.
- All supplemental information shall be filed by the applicant five days after publication of these rules in the Federal Register.
- The supplemental information must be certified and include the following: (a) Applicant's name; (b) Mailing Address (no Post Office boxes); (c) City; (d) State; (e) ZIP Code; (f) Auction Number 15; (g) FCC Account Number; (h) Person(s) authorized to make or withdraw a bid (list up to three individuals); (i) Certifications and name and title of person certifying the information provided; (j) Applicant's contact person and such person's telephone number, E-mail address and FAX number; and (k) Signature and date.
