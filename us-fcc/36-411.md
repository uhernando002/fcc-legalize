§ 36.411 Operating taxes—Account 7200. This account includes the taxes arising from the operations of the company, i.e.: (a) Operating Investment Tax Credits. (b) Operating Federal Income Taxes. (c) Operating State and Local Income Taxes. (d) Operating Other Taxes. (e) Provision for Deferred Operating Income Taxes. [83 FR 63587, Dec. 11, 2018]
