## Full Text

§ 27.1506 Frequencies. Link to an amendment published at 91 FR 15912, Mar. 31, 2026. The 897.5-900.5 MHz and 936.5-939.5 MHz band segments are available for licensing with an authorized bandwidth up to 3 megahertz paired channels. The 897.5-900.5 MHz segment must only be used for uplink transmissions. The 936.5-939.5 MHz segments must only be used for downlink transmissions.
