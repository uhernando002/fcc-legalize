## Full Text

§ 80.167 Limitations on operators. The operator of maritime radio equipment other than T-1, T-2, T, or G licensees must not: (a) Make equipment adjustments which may affect transmitter operation; (b) Operate any transmitter which requires more than the use of simple external switches or manual frequency selection or transmitters whose frequency stability is not maintained by the transmitter itself.
