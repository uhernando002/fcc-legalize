## Full Text

§ 73.6002 Licensing requirements. Link to an amendment published at 91 FR 2874, Jan. 23, 2026. (a) A Class A television broadcast license will only be issued to a qualified low power television licensee that: (1) Filed a Statement of Eligibility for Class A Low Power Television Station Status on or before January 28, 2000, which was granted by the Commission; and (2) Files an acceptable application for a Class A Television license (FCC Form 2100, Schedule F).
