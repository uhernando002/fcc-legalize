## Important Information

- (a) Licensees of voluntary ships are not required to maintain radio station logs.
- (b) When a ship radio station of a voluntary ship is being operated, the appropriate general purpose watches must be maintained in accordance with §§ 80.147 and 80.310.

## Full Text

§ 80.1153 Station log and radio watches. (a) Licensees of voluntary ships are not required to maintain radio station logs. (b) When a ship radio station of a voluntary ship is being operated, the appropriate general purpose watches must be maintained in accordance with §§ 80.147 and 80.310. [73 FR 4492, Jan. 25, 2008]
