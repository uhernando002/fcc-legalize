## Important Information

- The operator of a MURS station may use it for the purposes listed in this section.
- (a) MURS stations may be used to transmit voice, data or image signals.
- (b) MURS stations may be used for telecommand and telemetry functions.

§ 95.2731 Permissible MURS uses. The operator of a MURS station may use it for the purposes listed in this section. (a) MURS stations may be used to transmit voice, data or image signals. (b) MURS stations may be used for telecommand and telemetry functions.
