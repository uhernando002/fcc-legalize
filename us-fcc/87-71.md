## Important Information

- A licensed operator must measure the operating frequencies of all land-based transmitters at the following times: (a) When the transmitter is originally installed; (b) When any change or adjustment is made in the transmitter which may affect an operating frequency; or (c) When an operating frequency has shifted beyond tolerance.

## Full Text

§ 87.71 Frequency measurements. A licensed operator must measure the operating frequencies of all land-based transmitters at the following times: (a) When the transmitter is originally installed; (b) When any change or adjustment is made in the transmitter which may affect an operating frequency; or (c) When an operating frequency has shifted beyond tolerance.
