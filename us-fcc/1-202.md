## Important Information

- § 1.202 Official reporter; transcript.
- The Commission will designate an official reporter for the recording and transcribing of hearing proceedings as necessary.
- Transcripts will be transmitted to the Secretary for inclusion in the Commission's Electronic Comment Filing System.
