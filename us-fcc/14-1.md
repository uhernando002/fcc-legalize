§ 14.1 Applicability. Link to an amendment published at 91 FR 20374, Apr. 16, 2026. Except as provided in §§ 14.2, 14.3, 14.4 and 14.5 of this chapter, the rules in this part apply to: (a) Any manufacturer of equipment used for advanced communications services, including end user equipment, network equipment, and software, that such manufacturer offers for sale or otherwise distributes in interstate commerce; (b) Any provider of advanced communications services that such provider offers in or affecting interstate commerce.
