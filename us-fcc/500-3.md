## Full Text

§ 500.3 NTIA duty to review FirstNet proposed fees. As required under 47 U.S.C. 1428(c), NTIA shall exclusively review fees, which must be proposed by FirstNet in writing, through NTIA's review and approval process conducted on an annual basis.
