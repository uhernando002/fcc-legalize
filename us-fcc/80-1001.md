## Full Text

§ 80.1001 Applicability. The Bridge-to-Bridge Act and the regulations of this part apply to the following vessels in the navigable waters of the United States: (a) Every power-driven vessel of 20 meters or over in length while navigating; (b) Every vessel of 100 gross tons and upward carrying one or more passengers for hire while navigating; (c) Every towing vessel of 7.8 meters (26 feet) or over in length, measured from end to end over the deck excluding sheer, while navigating; and (d) Every dredge and floating plant engaged, in or near a channel or fairway, in operations likely to restrict or affect navigation of other vessels. An unmanned or intermittently manned floating plant under the control of a dredge shall not be required to have a separate radiotelephone capability.
