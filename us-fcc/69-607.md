## Citation

- Section: § 69.607
- Heading: Disbursement of Carrier Common Line residue
- Hierarchy: Title 47—Telecommunication > CHAPTER I—FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION > SUBCHAPTER B—COMMON CARRIER SERVICES > PART 69—ACCESS CHARGES > Subpart G—Exchange Carrier Association

## Important Information

- (a) The association shall compute a monthly net balance for each member telephone company that is not an average schedule company.
- If such a company has a negative net balance, the association shall bill that amount to such company.
- If such a company has a positive net balance, the association shall disburse that amount to such company.
- (b) The net balance for such a company shall be computed by multiplying a hypothetical net balance for such a company by a factor that is computed by dividing the Carrier Common Line residue by the sum of the hypothetical net balances for such companies.
- (c) The hypothetical net balance for each company shall be the sum of the hypothetical net balances for each access element.
- Such hypothetical net balances shall be computed in accordance with §§ 69.608 to 69.610.
