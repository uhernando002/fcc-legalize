## Full Text

§ 95.923 CBRS station inspection. If an authorized FCC representative requests to inspect a CBRS station, the operator must make the station and any station records available for inspection. (a) A CBRS station includes all of the equipment used in connection with that station. (b) Station records include the following documents, as applicable: (1) A copy of each response to an FCC violation notice or an FCC letter. (2) Each written permission received from the FCC.
