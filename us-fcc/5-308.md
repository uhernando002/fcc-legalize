## Full Text

§ 5.308 Stop buzzer. A “Stop Buzzer” point of contact must be identified and available at all times during operation of each experiment conducted under a program license. A “stop buzzer” point of contact is a person who can address interference concerns and cease all transmissions immediately if interference occurs. [78 FR 25162, Apr. 29, 2013]
