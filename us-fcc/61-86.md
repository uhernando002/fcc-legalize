## Full Text

§ 61.86 Supplements. An issuing carrier may not file a supplement except to suspend or cancel a tariff publication, or to defer the effective date of pending tariff revisions. A carrier may file a supplement for the voluntary deferral of a tariff publication. [76 FR 43216, July 20, 2011]
