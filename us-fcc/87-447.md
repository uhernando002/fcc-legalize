§ 87.447 Supplemental eligibility. An applicant for an operational fixed station must certify that: (a) The applicant is the licensee of an aeronautical land station in the aeronautical mobile service; and (b) Common carrier facilities are not available to satisfy the aeronautical station's requirements.
