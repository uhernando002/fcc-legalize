## Important Information

- § 80.37 One authorization for a plurality of stations.
- One station license may be issued to authorize a designated maximum number of marine utility stations operating at temporary unspecified locations, normally in multiples of ten stations when: (a) The licensee of each station is the same; and (b) The authorized area of operation of each station is the same.
