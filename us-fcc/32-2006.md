## Important Information

- (a) This account shall include the company's investment in regulated property which is not includable in the plant accounts as operating telecommunications plant.
- It shall include the company's investment in telecommunications property held for sale.
- (Note also Account 1406, Nonregulated Investments.) (b) Subsidiary records shall be maintained to show the character of the amounts carried in this account.
