§ 80.854 Radiotelephone installation. The radiotelephone installation includes: (a) A radiotelephone transmitter; (b) A receiver as specified in § 80.858(a); (c) A main source of energy; (d) A reserve source of energy, when required by § 80.860(a); (e) An antenna system.
