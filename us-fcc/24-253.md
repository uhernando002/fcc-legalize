## Full Text

§ 24.253 Termination of cost-sharing obligations. The cost-sharing plan will sunset for all PCS entities on April 4, 2005, which is ten years after the date that voluntary negotiations commenced for A and B block PCS entities. Those PCS entities that are paying their portion of relocation costs on an installment basis must continue the payments until the obligation is satisfied. [61 FR 29693, June 12, 1996]
