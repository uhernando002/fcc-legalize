## Important Information

- An open video system operator may offer video and local exchange services for sale in a single package at a single price, provided that: (a) The open video system operator, where it is the incumbent local exchange carrier, may not require that a subscriber purchase its video service in order to receive local exchange service; and (b) Any local exchange carrier offering such a package must impute the unbundled tariff rate for the regulated service.
