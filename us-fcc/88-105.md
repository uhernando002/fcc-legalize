§ 88.105 Emission mask. Transmitters operating in the 5030-5091 MHz band must comply with the ARS and GRS radio transmitter power spectral density (PSD) limits specified in technical standard RTCA DO-362A (incorporated by reference, see § 88.117) section 2.2.1.6.2.1.
