## Full Text

§ 64.4000 Basis and purpose. (a) Basis. The rules in this subpart are issued pursuant to the Communications Act of 1934, as amended. (b) Purpose. The purpose of these rules is to facilitate the timely and accurate establishment, termination, and billing of customer telephone service accounts.
