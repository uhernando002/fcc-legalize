## Important Information

- A notice of the proposed issuance, amendment, or repeal of a rule will include the following: (a) A statement of the time, nature and place of any public rulemaking proceeding to be held.
- (b) Reference to the authority under which the issuance, amendment or repeal of a rule is proposed.
- (c) Either the terms or substance of the proposed rule or a description of the subjects and issues involved.
- (d) The docket number assigned to the proceeding.
- (e) A statement of the time for filing comments and replies thereto.

§ 1.413 Content of notice. A notice of the proposed issuance, amendment, or repeal of a rule will include the following: (a) A statement of the time, nature and place of any public rulemaking proceeding to be held. (b) Reference to the authority under which the issuance, amendment or repeal of a rule is proposed. (c) Either the terms or substance of the proposed rule or a description of the subjects and issues involved. (d) The docket number assigned to the proceeding. (e) A statement of the time for filing comments and replies thereto.
