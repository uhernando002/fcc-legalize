## Important Information

- § 1.1410 Use of presumptions in calculating the space factor.
- With respect to the formulas referenced in § 1.1406(d)(1) and (d)(2), the space occupied by an attachment is presumed to be one foot.
- The amount of usable space is presumed to be 13.5 feet.
- The amount of unusable space is presumed to be 24 feet.
- The pole height is presumed to be 37.5 feet.
- These presumptions may be rebutted by either party.
