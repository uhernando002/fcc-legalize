## Full Text

§ 73.6017 Class A TV station protection of Class A TV stations. An application to change the facilities of a Class A TV station will not be accepted if it fails to protect authorized Class A stations in accordance with the requirements of § 74.793 (b) through (d), (g), and (j) of this chapter. This protection must be afforded to applications for changes in other authorized Class A stations filed prior to the date the Class A application is filed. [91 FR 2874, Jan. 23, 2026]
