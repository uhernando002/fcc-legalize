- Whenever a station is to transmit from unspecified locations within a prescribed geographical area, the station location is temporary unspecified and the proposed geographical operating area must be shown on the application.

## Full Text

§ 80.39 Authorized station location. This section describes the circumstances under which a coast station location is classified as permanent or temporary unspecified. (a) Permanent. Whenever a station is to transmit from a single location, the station location is permanent and the location must be shown on the application. (b) Temporary unspecified. Whenever a station is to transmit from unspecified locations within a prescribed geographical area, the station location is temporary unspecified and the proposed geographical operating area must be shown on the application.
